LLM evaluation for QA engineers

A practical introduction to groundedness, correctness, and repeatable AI quality checks.

1. Define what good means

Specify the task, acceptable behavior, safety constraints, latency, and cost budgets.

2. Create a representative dataset

Collect typical, ambiguous, adversarial, and edge-case prompts. Keep expected facts and rubric criteria.

3. Evaluate multiple dimensions

Measure task success, factuality, groundedness, tool correctness, safety, and consistency separately.

4. Use human review wisely

Calibrate model-based graders against human judgments and inspect disagreement cases.

5. Track regressions

Version prompts, datasets, models, and scores. Compare against a baseline before deployment.

Key takeaway

A fluent answer is not necessarily a correct or grounded answer.
