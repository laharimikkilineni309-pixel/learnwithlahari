Answer senior QA interview questions with evidence

Use a simple structure to explain ownership, technical decisions, and impact.

1. Describe the situation

Set context: application, risk, release constraints, and your responsibility.

2. Explain your decision

Why this test strategy, tool, or automation scope? Name tradeoffs.

3. Show the work

Mention test data, architecture, CI integration, debugging, and collaboration.

4. Quantify only what you know

Use verified metrics, or explain qualitative impact without inventing numbers.

5. Reflect

Say what you learned and what you would change next time.

Key takeaway

Strong interview answers show judgment, not just a list of tools.
