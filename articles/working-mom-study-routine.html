A flexible study routine for working moms

Use anchors, not rigid schedules, to keep learning sustainable.

1. Set two study anchors

Pick a primary window and a backup window that fit your household, without assuming every day is identical.

2. Match tasks to energy

Use focused windows for coding and lower-energy windows for reading or reviewing flashcards.

3. Create a minimum viable day

A 10-minute review counts. Resume the larger plan when you can.

4. Ask for support where possible

Coordinate protected study time with a partner, family member, or caregiver if available.

5. Review weekly, without guilt

Keep what worked, drop what did not, and plan one realistic next step.

Key takeaway

A flexible plan is a strong plan.
