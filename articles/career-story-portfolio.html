MotherhoodCareer· 8 min read

Your career story is more than a résumé

Translate technical work and life experience into a compelling, authentic portfolio.

1. Show your work

Write short case studies about a testing challenge, your approach, tradeoffs, and outcome.

2. Make projects easy to explore

Link to code, a README, sample test reports, and what you would improve.

3. Share learning honestly

Describe what you are exploring rather than claiming expertise you have not yet developed.

4. Define your own boundaries

Share only the personal details and family stories you are comfortable making public.

Key takeaway

You can be both technically ambitious and intentional about your personal life.
