Behind the learning

Hi, I'm Lahari.

Engineer. Lifelong learner. Mom. I believe we can grow in technology without pretending life happens in perfectly scheduled blocks.

Why I created Learn With Lahari

My professional interests span software quality engineering, test automation, APIs, and the rapidly evolving world of AI. I wanted one place to turn complex ideas into approachable, practical lessons—and to share what it looks like to keep learning while navigating motherhood.

Here you'll find testing guides grounded in real engineering principles, explorations of how to evaluate AI systems, and personal reflections on career growth, priorities, and the power of starting small.

What to expect

Clear explanations, hands-on examples, honest learning notes, and a welcoming space for beginners and experienced engineers alike. I'll distinguish tested techniques from ideas I'm still exploring, and I won't pretend there is one perfect path.

Let's learn together

Start with a guide, try the examples, and adapt them to your own journey. Questions, feedback, and thoughtful conversations are always welcome.
